# Brain External Research Dispatch Contract

Use when Brain needs external facts before PRD, routing, stage, or scope decisions can be made.

Packet title:

Brain Dispatch: External Research

Required Core Packet fields:
- Route
- Objective / Brain Intent
- Continuation
- Allowed Scope
- Forbidden Scope / Out of Scope
- Acceptance Criteria
- Verification Method
- Expected Evidence
- Authoritative Inputs
- Constraints
- Stop Conditions
- Expected Result

Required research fields:
- Research Goal
- Question
- Why it matters
- Preferred sources
- Out of scope
- Expected output

Rules:
- Ask for facts, examples, constraints, standards, or source-backed findings.
- Do not ask the research agent to make product decisions owned by Brain.
- Keep the request narrow.
- Include source expectations when source quality matters.
- Brain must clarify or reject an incomplete, ambiguous, or contradictory packet before dispatch.
